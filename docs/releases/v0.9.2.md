## ClipItTo 0.9.2: 4 bug fixes and 1 improvement

**Bug fixes (4)**
- #58 **Ctrl+E now adds or edits a note straight from the search box**, where the popup starts. Before, it did nothing until you moved down and up.
- #59 **A single word is no longer translated twice**: "hello" gave "שלום שלום" and "שלום" gave "Peaceful peace." because the model was trained on sentences. For one to three words the first candidate that doesn't repeat itself is taken.
- #61 **Searching a word with several matches** now selects the newest unsaved match when the start focus is "Unsaved history" (it used to pick the best-ranked one, often the second entry).
- #62 **Groups show on the first open after login** (they were scrolled out of sight until you pressed Up).

**Improvement (1)**
- #60 **Ctrl+T shows a translation without pasting it**, and the model's output for a short text is now capped by the text's length, so it can't run on for hundreds of pieces.

Also: the CI keeps build files only a day on branches and PRs, so the free storage quota stops filling up (#67).
