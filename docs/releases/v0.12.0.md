## ClipItTo 0.12.0: 1 bug fix, 2 improvements

**Bug fixes (1)**
- **The popup starts on the newest copy.** With the "Unsaved history" start focus the selection now lands on the top of Recent (it
  could land on a saved copy), or on the top search result. Search results of every kind are listed newest first.

**Improvements (2)**
- **Search inside pictures.** ClipItTo reads the text in each copied picture in the background, on your computer, and keeps it with
  the picture, so typing a word finds the screenshot that shows it (also together with `img:`). Pictures copied before are read a few
  seconds after the start. Settings › *Search inside pictures* turns it off and deletes the text that was read.
- **The keyring is asked only when needed, and says why.** The key that recognises a copied password is read from the keyring the first
  time a password is copied or searched, not at every start, and a notification says what ClipItTo is asking for before the keyring
  may show its own unlock window. `clipitto --status` has a new `keyring` line.
