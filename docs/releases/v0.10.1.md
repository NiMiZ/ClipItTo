## ClipItTo 0.10.1: 3 bug fixes

**Bug fixes (3)**
- **The Windows installer can replace a running ClipItTo.** It asked the app to quit and waited one second; a daemon that was busy (for
  example with a stuck translation) stayed, and the installer failed with "file in use" until the user ended `clipitto-daemon.exe` by hand.
  The installer (and the uninstaller) now ask it to quit, wait up to five seconds, and then end it.
- **ClipItTo always exits when told to.** The daemon now ends five seconds after a quit request even if a worker thread cannot be
  stopped (a translation stuck in the engine used to keep the process alive).
- **ClipItTo has an icon on Windows.** The Start menu, search and shortcuts showed a blank white window. The programs and the installer now
  carry the ClipItTo icon (Windows may take a moment, or a restart of Explorer, to refresh its icon cache).
