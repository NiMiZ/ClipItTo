## ClipItTo 0.13.0: 1 bug fix, 3 improvements

**Bug fixes (1)**
- **Ctrl+G, Ctrl+S and Ctrl+P work from the search box.** The popup opens with the cursor in the search box, where only Ctrl+E
  worked; the group, star and pin shortcuts did nothing until you moved into the list. They now act on the copy selected below.

**Improvements (3)**
- **Paste with formatting.** When a copy has formatting (bold, links, lists, tables, headings), ClipItTo also keeps its formatted
  version, and Shift+Enter › *With formatting* pastes it as it was, with the plain text alongside. Plain paste is still the default
  and is what Enter does. Settings › *Keep formatting of copied text* turns it off and deletes what was kept; copies marked by a
  password manager never keep any. On GNOME, log out and in once so the updated extension is used.
- **Colours and files in the list.** A copy that is one colour (`#ff8800`, `rgb(255, 136, 0)`...) shows a swatch of it. A list of
  files shows a file icon and the names, and files copied in Windows Explorer or macOS Finder are recorded as their paths. The files
  are never opened or read.
- **Show as QR code.** Right-click a text copy › *Show as QR code…* shows it as a QR code to scan with your phone (a link, Wi-Fi
  details, a short note). It is drawn on your computer and goes nowhere else; about 2,900 characters fit. Not offered for passwords.
