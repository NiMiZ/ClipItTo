## ClipItTo 0.15.0: auto grouping, and fewer stalls

**New (2)**
- **Auto grouping (off by default).** Settings › Groups has a new checkbox, *File new copies in the group they match*. When it is on,
  ClipItTo learns from what you move into your groups and files new copies in the group they match best: by a link's site and first
  folder, a path's folders, a command's first word, and the words of the text. It only uses groups you made, never creates one, and
  leaves a copy in Recent when no group clearly matches. A filed copy shows a small *auto* tag; moving it elsewhere teaches it the other
  way. Settings shows how many examples each group has, and *Forget what was learned* clears them. Passwords and pictures are never
  filed. Everything stays on your computer, in the history file.
- **Show a translation without pasting it.** The *Translate* submenu now has *Show the translation only* (the same as Ctrl+T), after the
  language pack.

**Improvements (2)**
- **The same text with Windows and Unix line ends is one entry on Linux.** Copies are stored with LF line ends, as on Windows and macOS.
  Entries copied before this are unchanged.
- **Translation stalls.** A very long text (over 10,000 characters) is refused at once instead of tying up the translator, a stuck
  translation can be cancelled between sentences, and choosing the language pack no longer scans a huge copy.

**Bug fixes (3)**
- **A clipboard owner that stops answering no longer freezes capture** on Wayland (KDE, Sway and others using wl-paste): ClipItTo gives
  up on it after a few seconds.
- **Restoring a backup is fast.** 3,000 copies took 9 seconds and a large history could take minutes; now 3,000 take under a second.
- **The popup no longer reads the whole history** each time it refreshes (190 ms with 50,000 copies, now almost nothing).
